About

تعارف

I build brands that are modern and culturally rich. The kind you look at and know where they come from.

I’m a brand designer and art director in Lahore. Engineer by training, illustrator in a past life, designer by choice.

My best work comes from really understanding who I’m designing for. I’ve been Streetfeet’s brand lead for two years. I know what it stands for and what it wants to say, so the work stays true to it.

Function comes first. But I want things to be beautiful too, and there has to be something of me in them. I can’t just copy or generate something and call it done. Right now so much design is drifting toward the same soulless look. I’d rather help brands hold on to who they are.
